Fixtures to Spond

Paste a season fixture list copied from FA Full-Time. Get a block you can paste straight into Spond's season planner to create availability requests, plus a calendar file.

No fixtures loaded

1. Paste the fixture list

Select the fixture rows on Full-Time, copy, and paste here. Headers and blank lines are ignored.

2. Set it up

Full-Time lists rarely carry kick-off times, so set your default here and correct any exceptions in the table.

Title and description accept {opponent} {home} {away} {ha} {venue} {comp} {team} {date}. A row with no time on Full-Time gets "kick-off to be confirmed" added to its description.

3. Check and correct

Everything here is editable, and edits carry through to every export. Untick a row to leave it out.

H/A Date Meet Kick-off Title Location

4. Send it to Spond

In Spond on the web, open your group, choose Event, then Plan your season, then Availability Request. Click into the first cell of the grid and paste.

Column order above matches Spond's season planner grid. If your grid differs, untick or reorder using the arrows and copy again. Response deadlines and reminders are set in Spond after the events are created, using bulk edit.
